📸 Generate Your OG Image

  1. Open this page in your browser (preferably Chrome)
  2. Right-click on the card below and select "Inspect" or press F12
  3. In DevTools, right-click on the <div id="og-card"> element
  4. Select "Capture node screenshot"
  5. Save the image as og-image.png in your public folder
  6. Deploy your changes and test with Twitter Card Validator
