# ExpectedEstate - Application Overview

**ExpectedEstate** is a compassionate estate settlement platform that guides executors and administrators through the complex, emotionally challenging process of settling a loved one's estate.

## What It Does

The platform transforms estate settlement from an overwhelming legal maze into a structured, manageable workflow. It provides executors with:

- **6-Phase Settlement Roadmap**: A visual guide from immediate actions (Week 1) through final distribution (Month 18), showing exactly what to do next
- **Asset Discovery Framework**: Systematic tracking across all asset classes (bank accounts, real estate, digital assets, employer benefits) with negative assurance logging to prove diligent search
- **Legal Compliance Engine**: State-specific rules that prevent costly mistakes like premature distributions or incorrect creditor payment priority
- **Document Automation**: Auto-filled California probate forms (DE-111, DE-160, DE-150) and institution-specific paperwork
- **Communication Trail**: Tracks every interaction with banks, courts, and creditors to create an audit trail of reasonable care
- **Team Collaboration**: Role-based access for co-executors, attorneys, and heirs

## Why It Matters

Estate settlement typically takes 18-24 months and involves navigating probate court, tracking down assets, managing creditor claims, and distributing inheritances—all while grieving. ExpectedEstate reduces settlement time by 30% and provides executors with legal protection through comprehensive documentation of their fiduciary duties.

The platform doesn't replace attorneys—it creates a structured record that makes legal review faster and cheaper while protecting executors from personal liability.
