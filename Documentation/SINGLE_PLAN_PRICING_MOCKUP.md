**"Is this really all I need?"**
→ Yes. We've included every feature you need to settle an estate confidently. No add-ons required.

→ This plan covers 98% of estates. If you have unique needs, contact us for custom solutions.

**"Why so cheap compared to attorneys?"**
→ Software scales. We can serve thousands of executors at a fraction of attorney costs.

**"Will the price go up?"**
→ Your price is locked in as long as you maintain your subscription.

## Future Considerations

### When to Add Tiers?

**Don't add tiers if:**
- Conversion rate is healthy (>20%)
- Support burden is manageable
- Feature adoption is high
- Customer feedback is positive

**Consider tiers if:**
- Enterprise customers need custom features
- Support costs are too high for all users
- Feature bloat is confusing users
- Revenue growth plateaus

### Potential Future Tiers

**Basic ($29/mo)** - For very simple estates
- Remove: Claims Engine, AI Discovery, Forms

**Enterprise (Custom)** - For attorneys/firms
- Add: Multi-user, White-label, API access
