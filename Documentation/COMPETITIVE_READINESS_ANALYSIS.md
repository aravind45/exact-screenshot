## Competitive Landscape Overview

### Market Segmentation

The estate settlement market has **three distinct segments**:

**Segment 1: Law Firms & Professionals (B2B)**
- Target: Estate attorneys, trust companies, financial advisors
- Price: $200-500/user/month ($4,000-10,000/year for firms)
- Players: Estateably, WealthCounsel, LEAP, Clio
- Market Size: 50,000 professionals × $5,000 avg = $250M

**Segment 2: DIY Executors (B2C)**
- Target: Individual executors settling estates
- Price: $29-99/month ($348-1,188/year)
- Players: **YOU**, EstateExec, (gap in market)
- Market Size: 1.4M executors × $500 avg = $700M

**Segment 3: Estate Planning (Pre-Death)**
- Target: Individuals planning their estates
- Price: $99-399 one-time
- Players: Trust & Will, LegalZoom, Everplans, Quicken WillMaker
- Market Size: 5M users × $200 avg = $1B

**Your Position:** Segment 2 (DIY Executors) - **Underserved market**
