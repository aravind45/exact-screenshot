# Forms Coverage Quick Reference

## ✅ What's Covered

### Full Probate (With Will)
- ✅ Petition for Probate
- ✅ Notice to Heirs/Creditors
- ✅ Letters Testamentary
- ✅ Inventory & Appraisal
- ✅ Creditor Claims
- ✅ Accounting
- ✅ Final Distribution
- ✅ Estate Closing

**All 4 states: CA, NY, TX, FL**

### Full Probate (No Will)
- ✅ Petition for Administration
- ✅ Letters of Administration
- ✅ All other probate forms

**All 4 states: CA, NY, TX, FL**

### Small Estate Procedures
- ✅ CA: Small Estate Affidavit
- ✅ NY: Voluntary Administration
- ✅ TX: Small Estate Affidavit + Muniment of Title
- ✅ FL: Summary Administration

### Spousal Property
- ✅ CA: Spousal Property Petition
- ✅ FL: Homestead Property Petition
- ⚠️ NY/TX: Not needed (automatic transfer)

---

## ❌ What's NOT Covered (And Why)

### Tax Forms
- ❌ IRS Form 706 (Estate Tax)
- ❌ State inheritance tax forms
- **Why**: These are IRS/state tax forms, not court forms
- **Solution**: Link to IRS website

### Real Estate Documents
- ❌ Deed preparation
- ❌ Title transfer documents
- ❌ Property tax reassessment
- **Why**: County recorder forms, not court forms
- **Solution**: Link to title company

### Trust Administration
- ❌ Trust amendments
- ❌ Trustee notices
- ❌ Trust accounting
- **Why**: Private documents, not court forms
- **Solution**: Separate trust module

### Non-Probate Transfers
- ❌ TOD/POD claim forms
- ❌ Life insurance claims
- ❌ Retirement account claims
- **Why**: These come from financial institutions
- **Solution**: Institution-specific guides
