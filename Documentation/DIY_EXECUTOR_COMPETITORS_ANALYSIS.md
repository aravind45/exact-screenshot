### The Market Gap

**98-99% of DIY executors are NOT using software!**

**Why?**
1. **Don't know it exists** - Low awareness
2. **Think it's too expensive** - Assume they need attorneys
3. **Think it's too complex** - Assume it's for professionals
4. **Use spreadsheets** - Default to Excel/Google Sheets
5. **Overwhelmed** - Don't know where to start

**The Opportunity:**

If you can capture just **5% of the DIY executor market**, that's:

If you can capture **10%**, that's:
