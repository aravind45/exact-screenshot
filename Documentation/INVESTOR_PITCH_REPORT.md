## The Problem

### 2.8 Million Americans Die Every Year
- 50% require probate (1.4M estates)
- Average estate value: $250K-$500K
- Average settlement time: 18-24 months
- Average executor stress level: **Overwhelming**

### Executors Are Drowning

**The Typical Executor:**
- First-time executor (90%)
- Grieving a parent or spouse
- Has a full-time job and family
- No legal or financial expertise
- Terrified of making a costly mistake

**Their Daily Reality:**
- 40+ institutions to contact (banks, brokerages, insurance, utilities)
- 100+ phone calls and emails
- 50+ documents to track
- 20+ deadlines to remember
- $5,000-$50,000 in attorney fees (if they can afford it)
- **18-24 months of stress and confusion**

### Current "Solutions" Don't Work

**Option 1: Hire an Attorney ($5,000-$50,000)**
- Pros: Expert guidance
- Cons: Expensive, slow, not always necessary
- **Problem:** 70% of executors can't afford this

**Option 2: DIY with Spreadsheets (Free)**
- Pros: Flexible, no cost
- Cons: No guidance, easy to lose track, no audit trail
- **Problem:** High error rate, missed deadlines, personal liability

**Option 3: Estate Planning Tools (Everplans, Trust & Will)**
- Pros: Document storage, will creation
- Cons: Pre-death focus, no settlement workflow
- **Problem:** Doesn't help AFTER someone dies

### The Market Gap

**No one is solving post-death estate settlement for the 70% of executors who can't afford attorneys.**

This is a $7 billion market opportunity (1.4M executors × $5,000 average spend).

## The Solution

### ExpectedEstate: Your AI-Powered Settlement Assistant

We guide executors through the entire estate settlement process with:

1. **Communication Command Center**
   - Track every call, email, and letter with 40+ institutions
   - AI-powered follow-up reminders
   - Searchable audit trail for legal protection
   - Never miss a deadline or lose track of progress

2. **Claims Priority Engine** ⭐ UNIQUE
   - Validates payment order per California Probate Code
   - Prevents executor personal liability
   - No competitor has this
   - Worth $10,000+ in mistake prevention

3. **Professional Form Generation**
   - Auto-fill California probate forms (DE-111, DE-160, DE-121, etc.)
   - Saves 10+ hours of data entry
   - Reduces court rejection risk
   - Eliminates $500-$2,000 in attorney fees per form

4. **Settlement Roadmap**
   - 6-phase visual workflow (Week 1 → Month 18)
   - 30+ actionable tasks with guidance
   - Progress tracking and completion percentages
   - Mobile-optimized for on-the-go access

5. **Asset Intelligence**
   - AI-powered document discovery (upload 1099, we detect Robinhood account)
   - Institution-specific workflows (Fidelity vs Vanguard)
   - Authority requirement detection (Letters vs Affidavit)
   - Freeze status tracking with DOD values

6. **Financial Dashboard**
   - Real-time estate value tracking
   - Beneficiary distribution calculator
   - Tax estimate (federal estate tax)
   - Net estate value (assets - debts)

### How It Works

**Week 1: Onboarding**
- Upload death certificate
- Add deceased information
- Import first 3 assets
- Set up probate tracking (if needed)

**Weeks 2-8: Discovery & Authority**
- AI scans documents for hidden assets
- Track down all accounts and property
- File probate petition (if needed)
- Obtain Letters of Administration

**Months 3-6: Asset Collection**
- Contact 40+ institutions systematically
- Track every communication
- Follow up automatically
- Collect and freeze all assets

**Months 7-12: Claims & Liquidation**
- Manage creditor claims with priority engine
- Pay debts in correct legal order
- Liquidate assets as needed
- File tax returns

**Months 13-18: Distribution & Closing**
- Calculate beneficiary shares
- Distribute assets with receipts
- File final accounting
- Close estate

**Result:** Settlement complete in 12-15 months (vs 18-24 industry average)
