# ExpectedEstate --- Product Hunt Launch (Personal Immigrant Story)

Growing up as an immigrant, I believed inheritance was automatic.

Living in the U.S. showed me how complex estate settlement really is.

After witnessing a close friend on an H1B visa pass away --- and later
helping my own family through California probate --- I built
ExpectedEstate.

ExpectedEstate provides structured, educational workflow guidance to
help executors stay organized.

Not legal advice. Just clarity during difficult moments.
