ENTERPRISE PROBATE MASTER Q&A (150+)

1.  What legally triggers probate? Answer: Death of an individual owning
    probate assets solely in their name.

2.  Which court handles probate? Answer: Probate or Surrogate Court in
    the decedent’s county of domicile.

3.  What establishes domicile? Answer: Primary residence plus intent to
    remain.

4.  Can probate be reopened? Answer: Yes, if newly discovered assets
    emerge.

5.  What invalidates a will? Answer: Lack of capacity, undue influence,
    fraud, improper execution.

6.  What is fiduciary duty? Answer: Highest legal duty requiring
    loyalty, prudence, and impartiality.

7.  Can an executor be personally liable? Answer: Yes, for negligence,
    self‑dealing, or improper distribution.

8.  What is estate marshaling? Answer: Securing and consolidating all
    probate assets.

9.  How are probate assets valued? Answer: Fair market value as of date
    of death.

10. What is step‑up in basis? Answer: Asset tax basis adjusts to fair
    market value at death.

11. What are exempt assets? Answer: Certain family allowances, homestead
    exemptions, and statutory allowances.

12. What is creditor priority order? Answer: Administrative costs →
    secured debts → funeral → taxes → unsecured debts.

13. What is a claim bar date? Answer: Statutory deadline for creditors
    to submit claims.

14. Can debts exceed estate assets? Answer: Yes; estate becomes
    insolvent and statutory priority controls.

15. What is ancillary probate? Answer: Secondary probate in another
    state for out‑of‑state real property.

16. How are LLC interests probated? Answer: Based on operating agreement
    transfer restrictions.

17. What happens to S‑Corp shares? Answer: Transfer must comply with
    shareholder agreement and eligibility rules.

18. Are digital assets probated? Answer: Yes if solely owned; access may
    require court authority.

19. What is RUFADAA? Answer: Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital
    Assets Act.

20. Can probate be contested? Answer: Yes via formal objection in
    probate court.

21. What is no‑contest clause? Answer: Provision discouraging
    beneficiaries from challenging the will.

22. How are minors protected? Answer: Guardianship or restricted
    accounts may be required.

23. What is a special needs trust? Answer: Trust preserving public
    benefits for disabled beneficiaries.

24. What is summary administration? Answer: Simplified probate for
    qualifying estates.

25. What defines a small estate threshold? Answer: State‑specific
    statutory dollar limit.

26. Do non‑citizens require probate? Answer: Yes if assets are located
    in U.S.

27. Are foreign beneficiaries taxed? Answer: Possible withholding and
    reporting requirements apply.

28. What happens if H1B holder dies? Answer: Estate proceeds under U.S.
    law; immigration status affects family residency.

29. Does life insurance go through probate? Answer: No if beneficiary
    designated.

30. Do retirement accounts pass through probate? Answer: No if valid
    beneficiary listed.

31. What is estate accounting? Answer: Detailed report of receipts,
    disbursements, and distributions.

32. Can executor take compensation? Answer: Yes, statutory or reasonable
    compensation allowed.

33. What delays probate most? Answer: Litigation, missing heirs, tax
    issues, real estate complications.

34. Is probate public? Answer: Generally yes; filings are public record.

35. Can probate be avoided entirely? Answer: Yes through comprehensive
    trust planning.

36. What is a pour‑over will? Answer: Will directing assets into a trust
    after death.

37. What if will is lost? Answer: Court may admit copy if validity
    proven.

38. What is bond requirement? Answer: Court‑ordered insurance protecting
    beneficiaries from executor misconduct.

39. When is bond waived? Answer: If will waives bond or court determines
    unnecessary.

40. What is estate income tax (Form 1041)? Answer: Tax return for income
    generated after death.

41. What is portability election? Answer: Transfer of unused federal
    estate tax exemption to surviving spouse.

42. How long must estate stay open? Answer: Until creditor period
    expires and assets distributed.

43. What is partial distribution? Answer: Early distribution before
    final accounting, if court allows.

44. What is fiduciary accounting standard? Answer: Uniform Principal and
    Income Act in many states.

45. What if heir cannot be located? Answer: Court may require heir
    search or escheat to state.

46. What is escheat? Answer: Transfer of assets to state when no heirs
    exist.

47. Can probate cross state lines? Answer: Yes through ancillary
    proceedings.

48. What are testamentary trusts? Answer: Trusts created under will upon
    death.

49. What if beneficiary dies during probate? Answer: Distribution
    follows anti‑lapse statute or beneficiary’s estate.

50. What is disclaimer of inheritance? Answer: Beneficiary legally
    refuses inheritance.

51. What is elective share? Answer: Spouse’s statutory right to claim
    portion despite will terms.
