### **1. Discovery of a Long‑Lost Will**
- A woman named Sarah finds a notarized will from her neighbor Joann, who died 10 years ago.
- Joann had no children and left all her assets to Sarah’s father (and to Sarah’s mother if her father had already passed).
- The estate was already settled years ago, so the discovery raises questions about what can be done now.

### **2. Probate Basics**
- When someone dies, their estate typically goes through probate to pay creditors and distribute assets.
- A valid will normally controls the distribution; without one, state intestacy laws apply.

### **3. What to Do After Finding an Old Will**
- **Contact an estate attorney immediately.**  
  Laws vary by state, and time limits may prevent reopening the estate.
- An attorney can determine whether the will can still be submitted or if the deadline has passed.

### **4. Research You Can Do Yourself**
- Probate records are usually public. You can search online or contact the county courthouse where the deceased lived.
- If real estate was involved, you can check county tax records to see who currently owns the property.
