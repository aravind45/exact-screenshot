## Pre-Recording Setup

### 1. Install Loom
- Go to [loom.com](https://www.loom.com)
- Sign up for free account
- Install Loom desktop app or Chrome extension
- Test your microphone and camera

### 2. Prepare Your Environment
- **Clean your browser**: Close unnecessary tabs
- **Test your app**: Open https://exact-screenshot-dusky.vercel.app/

### 3. Script Your Demo (2-3 minutes max)
Product Hunt users have short attention spans. Keep it tight and focused.

## Recording Tips

### Technical Settings
- **Recording mode**: Screen + Camera (picture-in-picture)
- **Camera position**: Bottom right corner
- **Screen resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame rate**: 30fps minimum
- **Audio**: Use external mic if possible (AirPods work well)

### Presentation Tips
1. **Speak clearly and enthusiastically** (but not over-the-top)
2. **Smile when on camera** (builds trust)
3. **Use cursor to guide attention** (circle important elements)
4. **Pause briefly between sections** (easier to edit)
5. **Keep mouse movements smooth** (no erratic clicking)
6. **Avoid "um" and "uh"** (practice your script)

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Talking too fast (slow down!)
- ❌ Going over 3 minutes (Product Hunt users will drop off)
- ❌ Too much technical jargon (keep it simple)
- ❌ Not showing your face (people connect with people)
- ❌ Poor audio quality (invest in a $20 mic if needed)
- ❌ Showing bugs or errors (test everything first!)

## Step-by-Step Recording Process

### 1. Open Loom
- Click Loom extension or desktop app
- Select "Screen + Camera"
- Choose "Full Screen" or "Current Tab"
- Position your camera bubble (bottom right recommended)

### 2. Do a Test Run
- Record 30 seconds
- Watch it back
- Check audio levels, camera position, screen clarity
- Adjust as needed

### 3. Record Your Demo
- Take a deep breath
- Click "Start Recording"
- Count to 3 before speaking (gives you buffer to edit)
- Follow your script
- Don't worry about small mistakes (you can trim later)
- End with a smile and pause for 2 seconds

### 4. Review and Trim
- Watch the full video
- Use Loom's trim tool to cut dead air at start/end
- Remove any long pauses or mistakes
- Add a custom thumbnail (use your hero image!)

### 5. Optimize for Sharing
- **Title**: "ExpectedEstate: Navigate Estate Settlement with Confidence"
- **Description**: 
  ```
  ExpectedEstate is a compassionate platform that guides executors through 
  the complex estate settlement process. From probate filing to final 
  distribution, we provide a 6-phase roadmap, automated forms, and legal 
  compliance checks that reduce settlement time by 30%.
  
  🚀 Live on Product Hunt: [link]
  🌐 Try it: https://exact-screenshot-dusky.vercel.app/
  ```
- **Thumbnail**: Upload your hero image from generate-launch-hero.html
- **Privacy**: Set to "Anyone with the link"

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Video is too long
- **Solution**: Cut the onboarding demo, focus on roadmap + one killer feature

**Problem**: Audio has echo
- **Solution**: Record in a smaller room, add soft materials (blankets, pillows)

**Problem**: Screen looks blurry
- **Solution**: Record at 1920x1080, ensure Loom quality is set to "High"

**Problem**: You sound nervous
- **Solution**: Do 3-5 practice runs, pretend you're explaining to a friend

**Problem**: Demo has bugs
- **Solution**: Use a pre-seeded test account, test the full flow before recording
