## Detailed Tier 4 Improvements

### ANCILLARY_PROBATE (68% → 85%) ✅

**Before**: 4 basic stages with no task details  
**After**: 5 detailed stages with 35 comprehensive tasks

**New Stages**:
1. **Domiciliary Probate Completion** (7 tasks)
   - Complete primary probate in home state
   - Obtain certified Letters Testamentary
   - Identify all out-of-state property
   - Determine which states require ancillary
   - Research each state's requirements
   - Estimate ancillary probate costs
   - Consider selling vs transferring property

2. **Exemplified Will & Documents** (6 tasks)
   - Obtain exemplified copy of Will
   - Obtain exemplified Letters Testamentary
   - Get certified death certificates (multiple)
   - Prepare authenticated court documents
   - Research local state filing requirements
   - Identify required local forms

3. **Local Counsel & Filing** (7 tasks)
   - Retain local attorney in property state
   - File Petition for Ancillary Probate
   - Submit exemplified Will and Letters
   - Appoint local personal representative (if required)
   - Pay local filing fees
   - Publish local creditor notice
   - Serve local heirs/beneficiaries

4. **Local Administration** (7 tasks)
   - Obtain local Letters Testamentary
   - Open local estate bank account
   - Pay local property taxes
   - Maintain property during probate
   - Resolve local creditor claims
   - Coordinate with domiciliary executor
   - File local tax returns (if required)

5. **Property Transfer or Sale** (9 tasks)
   - Decide: sell property or transfer to heirs
   - If selling: list property and accept offer
   - If selling: close sale
   - If transferring: prepare deeds to beneficiaries
   - Pay local closing costs
   - Transfer proceeds to domiciliary estate
   - File final local accounting
   - Close ancillary probate
   - Record final documents with county

**Key Features Added**:
- ✅ Multi-state coordination workflow
- ✅ Exemplified document requirements
- ✅ Local counsel retention process
- ✅ Property disposition options
- ✅ Domiciliary/ancillary coordination

### INSOLVENT (65% → 85%) ✅

**Before**: 4 basic stages with no task details  
**After**: 6 detailed stages with 52 comprehensive tasks

**New Stages**:
1. **Insolvency Determination** (7 tasks)
   - Inventory all estate assets
   - Appraise assets at fair market value
   - List all known debts and claims
   - Calculate total assets vs liabilities
   - Determine if estate is insolvent
   - Document insolvency analysis
   - Consult with probate attorney

2. **Creditor Priority Classification** (9 tasks)
   - Classify each debt by priority
   - Priority 1: Administration costs
   - Priority 2: Funeral and last illness
   - Priority 3: Family allowance
   - Priority 4: Secured creditors
   - Priority 5: Taxes (federal, state)
   - Priority 6: Medical bills (last 60 days)
   - Priority 7: General unsecured creditors
   - Priority 8: Lower priority claims

3. **Court Approval & Notice** (7 tasks)
   - File Notice of Insolvency with court
   - Request court approval for payment plan
   - Publish extended creditor notice (4 months)
   - Send notice to known creditors
   - Explain pro-rata distribution
   - Set claims deadline
   - File preliminary insolvency report

4. **Asset Liquidation** (8 tasks)
   - Obtain court approval to sell assets
   - Liquidate non-exempt assets
   - Conduct estate sales if needed
   - Sell real property (if authorized)
   - Collect all receivables
   - Close bank accounts
   - Deposit all funds in estate account
   - Maintain detailed liquidation records

5. **Pro-Rata Distribution** (9 tasks)
   - Calculate total funds available
   - Pay Priority 1 claims in full
   - Pay Priority 2 claims in full
   - Calculate pro-rata percentage
   - Prepare distribution schedule
   - Obtain court approval of distribution
   - Issue checks to approved creditors
   - Obtain releases from creditors
   - File proof of payment with court

6. **Final Accounting & Closure** (8 tasks)
   - Prepare final accounting
   - Document priority order compliance
   - File final report with court
   - Notify all creditors of final distribution
   - Explain why lower priority creditors received nothing
   - Request discharge as executor
   - Close estate bank account
   - Obtain court order closing estate

**Key Features Added**:
- ✅ Insolvency determination process
- ✅ 8-level creditor priority classification
- ✅ Court approval requirements
- ✅ Asset liquidation workflow
- ✅ Pro-rata distribution calculator
- ✅ Executor liability protection
- ✅ Final accounting and compliance
