# Marketing Ad Creatives for ExpectedEstate

## Overview
Create high-converting ad creatives for Google Ads and Facebook/Instagram that target executors in their moment of need.

---

## Target Audience Segments

### Segment 1: Newly Appointed Executors
**Demographics**: 35-65, any gender, middle-upper income
**Psychographics**: Overwhelmed, scared, searching for guidance
**Search intent**: "what does an executor do", "executor duties"
**Pain points**: "I have no idea where to start"

### Segment 2: Stuck Executors
**Demographics**: 40-70, any gender
**Psychographics**: Frustrated, behind schedule, considering hiring attorney
**Search intent**: "how long does probate take", "estate settlement timeline"
**Pain points**: "This is taking forever and I'm making mistakes"

### Segment 3: Liability-Concerned Executors
**Demographics**: 45-75, higher income, risk-averse
**Psychographics**: Anxious about personal liability
**Search intent**: "can executor be held liable", "executor mistakes"
**Pain points**: "What if I mess this up and get sued?"

## Ad Creative Specifications

### Google Display Ads
- **Sizes**: 300x250, 728x90, 160x600, 300x600, 970x250
- **File format**: JPG, PNG, GIF (static or animated)
- **File size**: <150KB
- **Text**: Headline (30 chars), Description (90 chars)

### Facebook/Instagram Feed Ads
- **Image size**: 1200x628 (1.91:1 ratio)
- **Video size**: 1080x1080 (1:1 ratio) or 1080x1920 (9:16 ratio)
- **File format**: JPG, PNG, MP4, MOV
- **Video length**: 15-60 seconds
- **Text**: Primary text (125 chars), Headline (40 chars)

### Instagram Story Ads
- **Size**: 1080x1920 (9:16 ratio)
- **File format**: JPG, PNG, MP4, MOV
- **Video length**: 15 seconds max
- **Text**: Minimal (use visuals)

### YouTube Ads
- **Size**: 1920x1080 (16:9 ratio)
- **File format**: MP4, MOV
- **Length**: 15-30 seconds (skippable), 6 seconds (bumper)
- **Audio**: Required

## Ad Copy Formulas

**Problem**: "Just named executor? No idea where to start?"
**Agitate**: "Most executors take 18 months and make costly mistakes."
**Solve**: "ExpectedEstate gives you a step-by-step roadmap. Try free."

### Formula 2: Before → After → Bridge
**Before**: "18 months of confusion, $15K in attorney fees"
**After**: "12 months with a roadmap, $29/month"
**Bridge**: "ExpectedEstate guides you through every step. Try free."

### Formula 3: Question → Answer → CTA
**Question**: "Can an executor be held personally liable?"
**Answer**: "Yes—if you pay debts in the wrong order or distribute too early."
