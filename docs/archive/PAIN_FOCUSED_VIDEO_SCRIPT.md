## The Story-Driven Approach
Show the executor's emotional journey from overwhelm to confidence. No feature lists—just solving real pain points.

## Recording Instructions

### Setup
1. **Create a persona**: Use a real name (Sarah, John, Maria) to make it relatable
2. **Use real screens**: Show actual app, not mockups
3. **Minimal talking head**: Show your face only at start and end (5-10 seconds total)
4. **Let the story breathe**: Pause between scenes for 1-2 seconds

### Tone
- **Empathetic**: You understand their pain
- **Calm**: You're the guide, not a salesperson
- **Confident**: You know this works
- **Human**: Tell a story, not a pitch

### Pacing
- **Slow down**: Speak 20% slower than normal
- **Pause**: Let emotional moments land
- **Breathe**: Don't rush through the pain points

---

## Key Principles

### 1. Show, Don't Tell
❌ "We have a 6-phase roadmap feature"
✅ "Sarah sees exactly what happens when—Week 1, Month 1, Month 3"

### 2. Name the Pain First
❌ "Our platform helps executors"
✅ "She's terrified. One mistake could cost her everything."

### 3. Use Specific Numbers
❌ "It saves time"
✅ "12 months instead of 18. She's done 6 months faster."

### 4. Show Consequences
❌ "We prevent mistakes"
✅ "It just saved her from a $50,000 mistake"

### 5. End with Emotion
❌ "Try our product today"
✅ "The heirs are happy. She's protected. That's ExpectedEstate."

## Pain Points to Hit (Pick 2-3)

1. **"I don't know where to start"** → Show roadmap giving instant clarity
2. **"I'm afraid of making mistakes"** → Show compliance warning preventing error
3. **"I can't find all the assets"** → Show systematic discovery checklist
4. **"I'm worried about liability"** → Show final dossier protecting them
5. **"This is taking forever"** → Show progress tracking and completion
6. **"I can't afford an attorney for everything"** → Show how it makes attorney review faster/cheaper

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't list features ("We have asset tracking, form generation, and...")
❌ Don't use jargon ("Our proprietary compliance engine...")
❌ Don't compare to competitors ("Unlike other solutions...")
❌ Don't oversell ("Revolutionary! Game-changing!")
❌ Don't show empty states (use populated demo data)
❌ Don't go over 90 seconds (60 is better)

## Pro Tips

### Make It Personal
Start with: "Three weeks ago, Sarah..." (not "Users often...")
Use real emotions: "She's terrified" (not "Users feel uncertain")

### Use Silence
After showing the overwhelm, pause for 2 seconds. Let it sink in.
After showing the protection, pause. Let them feel the relief.

### Show Real Reactions
If you can, show a real user's face (with permission) reacting to the app.
Or show your own face reacting: "Look at this—it just stopped her from..."

### End Strong
Don't fade out with "So yeah, check us out..."
End with impact: "That's ExpectedEstate. We're live today."
