## 🔍 Form Numbering Conflict Resolution

### The DE-315 Problem

**Your Current List**: DE-315 = "Order for Final Distribution"  
**Screenshot Shows**: DE-315 = "Order Determining Succession to Real Property"  
**Santa Clara County**: DE-315 = "Order Determining Succession to Real Property (Estates $100,000 or less)"

**Possible Explanations**:
1. California has TWO different DE-315 forms for different purposes
2. The form was updated/replaced and numbering changed
3. There's an error in one of the sources

**Action Required**:
1. Visit https://www.courts.ca.gov/forms.htm
2. Search for both versions of DE-315
3. Determine if both exist or if one is obsolete
4. Update your list accordingly

**Likely Resolution**: The "Order Determining Succession to Real Property" is the correct DE-315, and "Order for Final Distribution" may be a different form number (possibly DE-295 or a local form).

## 🎓 Lessons Learned

### For Future State Implementations
1. **Don't just copy the "most common" forms** - include companion forms
2. **Consider edge cases** - minors, incapacitated persons, contested estates
3. **Include waiver forms** - they speed up the process
4. **Verify form numbering** - check official sources, not just third-party sites
5. **Get complete form lists** - don't rely on "top 20" lists from blogs

### For California Completion
1. **Priority 1**: Add guardian ad litem forms (affects ~30% of estates with children)
2. **Priority 2**: Add special notice forms (used in almost all estates)
3. **Priority 3**: Add companion forms (spousal property order, etc.)
4. **Priority 4**: Add waiver forms (optional but useful)
5. **Priority 5**: Add specialized forms (contested, ancillary, etc.)
